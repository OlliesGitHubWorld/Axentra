Roadmap

What's being worked on now, what comes next and what's planned for later. Plans can change, so there are no dates.

Now

Being worked on

  • Discord integrationA built-in Discord bot: account linking, role sync, cases and reports in Discord, and staff chat in both directions.
  • Ban appeals in DiscordPlayers appeal with a form; staff accept, deny or reduce with a reason and can talk to the player through DMs.
  • Discord setup wizardChecks the bot's permissions and creates the missing channels for you.
  • Live server statusA Discord message with players, TPS and health that updates itself, plus start, stop and crash alerts.
  • Player info/afk, /seen, /whois, /near and a grouped /list.
  • Smarter cleanupClear mobs as well as items, protect death drops, per-world settings and instant leaf decay.

Next

Coming up after that

  • MySQL & MariaDBOptional MySQL storage with a one-command migration and automatic daily backups.
  • Network supportVelocity and BungeeCord: bans, mutes and staff chat across all servers, plus /find, /send and /glist.
  • Chat protectionA word filter that catches bypass tricks, anti-advertising and anti-spam, with escalating punishments.
  • /helpopPlayers ask staff for help; staff can answer in-game or from Discord.
  • PlaceholderAPI supportTrust, playtime, AFK, TPS and more as placeholders for other plugins.

Later

Planned, not started

  • Block logging & rollback
  • Web panel

Have an idea? Suggest it on Discord or GitHub.
